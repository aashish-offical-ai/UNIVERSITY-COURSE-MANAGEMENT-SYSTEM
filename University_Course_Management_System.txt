QUESTION 4
Get the number of students enrolled in each course, filtering for courses with more than 5 students.

SQL QUERY:
SELECT c.CourseID, c.CourseName, COUNT(DISTINCT e.StudentID) AS StudentCount
FROM Courses AS c
JOIN Enrollments AS e ON e.CourseID = c.CourseID
GROUP BY c.CourseID, c.CourseName
HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT e.StudentID) > 5;

MYSQL-STYLE OUTPUT:
+----------+---------------------+--------------+
| CourseID | CourseName          | StudentCount |
+----------+---------------------+--------------+
| 101      | Introduction to SQL | 12           |
+----------+---------------------+--------------+
1 row(s) in set

REASON:
COUNT(DISTINCT e.StudentID) counts unique students per course. GROUP BY creates a separate result for each course, and HAVING filters the groups to courses with more than five students.

QUESTION 16
Label students as Senior or Junior based on their year of enrollment.

SQL QUERY:
SELECT StudentID, FirstName, LastName, EnrollmentDate,
       CASE
           WHEN EnrollmentDate < DATE_SUB(CURDATE(), INTERVAL 4 YEAR)
               THEN 'Senior'
           ELSE 'Junior'
       END AS StudentLevel
FROM Students
ORDER BY StudentID;

OUTPUT:
+-----------+-----------+----------+----------------+--------------+
| StudentID | FirstName | LastName | EnrollmentDate | StudentLevel |
+-----------+-----------+----------+----------------+--------------+
| 1         | John      | Doe      | 2022-08-01     | Senior       |
| 2         | Jane      | Smith    | 2021-08-01     | Senior       |
| 3         | Amit      | Patel    | 2023-07-15     | Junior       |
| 4         | Sara      | Khan     | 2024-08-01     | Junior       |
| 5         | Ravi      | Shah     | 2020-08-01     | Senior       |
| 6         | Priya     | Mehta    | 2025-08-01     | Junior       |
| 7         | Neha      | Joshi    | 2022-07-20     | Senior       |
| 8         | Arjun     | Verma    | 2021-07-25     | Senior       |
| 9         | Mina      | Desai    | 2023-08-10     | Junior       |
| 10        | Kabir     | Singh    | 2019-08-01     | Senior       |
| 11        | Isha      | Rao      | 2024-07-15     | Junior       |
| 12        | Dev       | Joshi    | 2022-08-15     | Senior       |
+-----------+-----------+----------+----------------+--------------+
12 row(s) in set

REASON:
CASE checks whether EnrollmentDate is earlier than the date four years before CURDATE(). If it is, the student is labelled Senior; otherwise, Junior. The result depends on the date when the query is run.
